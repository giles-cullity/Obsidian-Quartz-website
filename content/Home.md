#### Giles Cullity's thought & picture database
- This site is both my personal notes and a demonstration of my system for organizing notes - "Life Argument Mapping".
- Life Argument Mapping is an alternative to the Zettelkasten method - it's a method of organizing notes for people who write down *thousands* of them. 
- It consists of all my thoughts over the years as well as my collection of favorite pictures/music/etc. I call my thoughts **declarative content** and the pictures etc **decorative content**.
- There are really 2 components to the system - a **multi-faceted navigation system** and an **argument mapping visualization**. The multi-facet navigation applies to both my thoughts and the pictures. The argument mapping only applies to the thoughts. 
- My thoughts can roughly be divided into thoughts about how the world works - **modelling**, what to do - **planning**, then thoughts about either of those - **meta**.
- Thoughts are also divided into **questions** and **opinions**. My system has *one question or opinion per note*. Those are the **content notes**. Then there are the **navigation notes**. Those are categories of opinions and questions. Those are like chapter names and sections of a book and are used to navigate to individual questions and opinions.
- Questions and opinions are primarily categorized by where they sit in the *reasoning process from observation to action*. I call this **argument stage**. ...

#### About this site
- ...
- This website is basically the personal notes that I actually use in my own life. It's like uploading my whole personal file directory to OneDrive and making that public. The main difference is that the organization is much more complex than a simple tree structure. And I'm only uploading notes once they're well written/organized. Some very private notes are going to be left out too.
- I'm still in the stage of uploading notes. As of October 2026 1000 notes have been uploaded. The final site will have **3000-10,000 notes**. That's why it has to be so precise and complex.
- The site was created using Obsidian Publish. Obsidian is a notetaking app like OneNote except much more feature rich. It has a feature where you can publish your notes as a website with a few clicks. That's how I got from "personal files" to "website".
#### How to use this site
- The site isn't designed to be easy to use. It's designed to convey a type of understanding that you can't get from sites that are easy to use: how everything fits together.
- At this stage it's best to navigate by **following the links in the tables**. Look for "personal navigation down" and "personal navigation up". You can also use other relationships in the tables as alternative navigations.
- **Canvases:** The main navigation method is going to be Obsidian Canvases. ... . These can't be uploaded directly. So I'm working on a workaround and hope to get them working by the end of 2026.
- **Tables:** The other way of navigating the site is using the links in the tables of the notes. ...
- Folders: The folders on the left are mainly for showing where a note sits on the scale from large to categories to individual opinions, questions and pictures. And giving a sense of how many categories are at each level and how many individual opinions, questions and pictures there are. It can't really be used to navigate the site directly.
- Search box: You can use the search box at the top left if there's a particular note or category that you're after and you know the name. Otherwise it can't really be used for navigation.
- Global Graph: the Global Graph view on the right is mainly for getting a sense of how many notes there are and how complex the organization is. It won't work well for actual navigation.
