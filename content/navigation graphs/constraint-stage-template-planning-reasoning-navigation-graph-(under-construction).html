  	- "Constraint" used interchangeably with "decision" here.
  	- This comes from looking for a word for "narrowing down the solution space". I wanted something to convey "taking some options out of the picture". I wanted something to match "consideration". It was out of "convergence" and "constraint" and I chose the latter.
  	- Usually constraints are part of the definition of the problem when making a decision. But when looking at multiple decisions, once one decision is made, that becomes a constraint in the next decision.
