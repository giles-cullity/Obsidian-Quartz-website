- **provisional description**:
	- This exercise is basically shoulder flexion/extension that mimics the action of the arms in sprinting
	- It's not a pushing/pulling movement like a close grip incline press or high row. It's not completely straight arm like a front dumbbell raise or straight arm pulldown either. ... . Any amount of elbow flexion/extension is allowed throughout the range of motion to get the weight to the top and back. In practice, I find myself using a bit of elbow flexion at the bottom, then having the forearm vertical or possibly even going into a bit of a press at the top. Trying to use more elbow angle than this doesn't feel natural and doesn't make the movement any easier. So strength varying with the amount of elbow angle isn't an issue.
	- ...
	- The flexion movement is done using a Bamboo bench pad to let the shoulder blades move properly.
	- I use a 50 degree incline on the bench (50 degree decline for the extension direction)
	- ...
