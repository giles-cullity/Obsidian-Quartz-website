- **provisional description**:

	- I came up with the Strength increments when I found myself thinking "I don't need to get that strong because I'm mobile". They're designed to get me to keep pushing strength when mobility has improved - not use mobility as an excuse to be weak. Then I thought other people would find the strength standards useful in the same way.
	- Note that the Strength increments aren't an actual program. They're not saying you should only use 6 reps and move through those weights in your training. You can use what ever rep ranges you want in your normal training. These are just benchmarks and a road map towards strength and athleticism goals.
