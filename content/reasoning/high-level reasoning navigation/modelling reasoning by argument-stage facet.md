- **other notes (provisional description):** I got the idea of the main argument stages from Marcus Hutter. I saw a Youtube clip of him breaking down the reasoning process of potential AI agents into Observation, Model, and Decision. Decision became "planning reasoning". (I then subdivided this into "consideration stage" and "constraint stage"). Model stage basically stayed the same. Observation turned into "data stage reasoning". This because I wanted to convey multiple steps in reasoning after the point of observation rather than the very first point or basic visual processing. I also formed the view that data sets were propositions. And they came at the start of the reasoning chain near where Marcus Hutter's Observation is. So Observation became "data set stage reasoning". Then I simplified it to "data stage reasoning".
