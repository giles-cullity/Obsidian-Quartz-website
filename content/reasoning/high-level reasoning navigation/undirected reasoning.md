- **provisional description**:
	- Reasoning where I'm not trying to figure out a specific thing. I'm doing it because it seems interesting or seems like it'll be useful later. But I don't know what I'll use it for at the time.
	- In contrast to planning reasoning where I'm trying to answer specific questions about what to do.
	- ...
	- I've called it "divergent reasoning" but I don't see it as being creative. I think creativity is about solving optimization problem using heuristics rather than first-principles reasoning. It's doing optimization without framing it as optimization. I think characterizing creativity in terms of divergent thinking is just confused. This reasoning is diverging from a single observation or change in opinion to multiple opinions about my model of the world. There's no creating happening because that happens after the model stage in planning.
