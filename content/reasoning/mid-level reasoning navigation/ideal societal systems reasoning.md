previous_note_body: "- ** : why this is consideration stage reasoning: aspects of e.g. the political system aren't things I can directly decide on - they're not control variables in my life. What are control variables for me, are decisions about what to write on my website. So I think in terms of where this fits into my reasoning system, all of what the ideal political system is is consideration stage reasoning - even the \"constraint stage\" reasoning about the ideal political system. E.g. if it's a \"constraint\" that the political system will have an objective function for a constitution instead of a set of prohibitions, that's actually a consideration in my life reasoning. Implementing the ideal political system is a consideration in my life. Writing about it and doing that well is also a consideration. It's not necessarily worth it when you think about how there are lots of other important things to do with time and energy. It turns out that I do think it does make the list of things I should do. So writing about it in general is a constraint. Within that, the consideration still applies though. There's still the question of exactly how much time and energy I spend on it for example. Making that decision is a matter of weighing up the importance (consideration) of an ideal political system being realized vs the importance of e.g. more development on the PKM project, realizing my potential in fitness, relationships, etc."
