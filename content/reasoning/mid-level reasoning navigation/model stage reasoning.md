  	- Internal logical structure thoughts:
  		- A model is more than just a set of correlations.
  		- So even after going from sample correlation (correlations among things I can observe) to populational correlation (correlation among all things under a definition), there are more logical steps involved with going from correlation to model.
  		- my view is that in terms of internal logical structure, models are conjunctions of conditionals. If A then B, AND if C then D, AND if E then F, etc. The A, C, E etc here - the antecedent conditions of each conditional conjunct - are the possible values of the *independent variable*, in equation format. The B, D, F, etc here - the consequent conditions in each conditional - are the corresponding values of the *dependent variable*.
